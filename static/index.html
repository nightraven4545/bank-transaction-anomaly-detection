How it works
  • Features: amount, duration, login attempts, amount ÷ balance, and amount ÷ the account's usual amount. Age and occupation are deliberately left out.
  • Detectors: Isolation Forest (easy to isolate), KNN (far from its neighbours) and LOF (sparser than its neighbourhood). Each score is standardised and the three are averaged.
  • Alerts: the top alert rate share of transactions, sized to review capacity instead of a fixed probability cut-off.
  • Privacy: uploaded files are scored in memory and never stored.
Sample data: Bank Transaction Dataset for Fraud Detection by Vala Khorasani (Apache-2.0). Inspired by DataCamp's Detecting Anomalous Transactions project. Code under the MIT License.
